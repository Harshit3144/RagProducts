Comprehensive Psychology Notes

1. Introduction
- Psychology is the scientific study of mind and behavior.
- It spans biological, cognitive, social, developmental, clinical, and applied domains.
- Depth psychology focuses on unconscious processes, but psychology as a whole integrates many perspectives.

2. Biological Psychology
- Brain structures: cortex, limbic system, brainstem.
- Neurotransmitters: dopamine, serotonin, GABA, acetylcholine.
- Genetics and heritability of traits.
- Psychophysiology: stress response, hormones, sleep cycles.

3. Cognitive Psychology
- Perception: how we interpret sensory input.
- Attention: selective focus, divided attention.
- Memory: short-term, long-term, working memory.
- Language: acquisition, comprehension, production.
- Problem-solving and decision-making.

4. Developmental Psychology
- Lifespan stages: infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, aging.
- Cognitive development: Piaget’s stages.
- Social development: attachment theory, Erikson’s psychosocial stages.
- Moral development: Kohlberg’s stages.
- Aging and cognitive decline.

5. Social Psychology
- Group dynamics: conformity, obedience, leadership.
- Attitudes and persuasion.
- Prejudice, stereotypes, discrimination.
- Interpersonal attraction and relationships.
- Social identity and cultural influences.

6. Personality Psychology
- Trait theories: Big Five (OCEAN).
- Psychodynamic theories: Freud, Jung.
- Humanistic theories: Maslow, Rogers.
- Behavioral and social learning approaches.
- Personality assessment methods.

7. Clinical Psychology
- Mental disorders: anxiety, depression, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder.
- Diagnostic systems: DSM, ICD.
- Therapy approaches: psychoanalysis, CBT, humanistic therapy, behavior therapy.
- Trauma and PTSD.
- Positive psychology interventions.

8. Depth Psychology
- Unconscious mind: personal vs collective unconscious.
- Archetypes: self, shadow, anima/animus, hero, mother, trickster.
- Dream analysis and symbolism.
- Active imagination and mythological study.
- Ecopsychology: human-nature connection.

9. Health Psychology
- Stress and coping mechanisms.
- Mind-body connection: psychosomatic illness.
- Lifestyle factors: exercise, diet, sleep.
- Chronic illness and psychological adjustment.
- Health promotion and prevention.

10. Industrial/Organizational Psychology
- Workplace motivation and productivity.
- Leadership styles and organizational culture.
- Job satisfaction and burnout.
- Personnel selection and training.
- Human factors and ergonomics.

11. Educational Psychology
- Learning theories: behaviorism, constructivism.
- Motivation in learning.
- Classroom management and pedagogy.
- Special education and inclusive practices.
- Assessment and evaluation.

12. Positive Psychology
- Well-being and happiness.
- Flow and engagement.
- Resilience and strengths.
- Gratitude and mindfulness.
- Meaning and purpose in life.

13. Research Methods in Psychology
- Experimental design: variables, control, randomization.
- Observational studies and case studies.
- Surveys and questionnaires.
- Statistical analysis: correlation, regression, ANOVA.
- Ethical considerations in research.

14. Applications of Psychology
- Clinical practice and therapy.
- Education and learning enhancement.
- Workplace and organizational improvement.
- Sports psychology and performance.
- Environmental psychology and sustainability.

15. Integration and Future Directions
- Neuroscience and psychology convergence.
- AI and cognitive modeling.
- Cross-cultural psychology.
- Ecopsychology and climate change.
- Holistic approaches combining biology, cognition, and depth psychology.
